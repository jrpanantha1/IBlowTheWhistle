<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Trust is the foundation of every successful organization, yet it is often in short supply. A key reason for this is the fear of reprisal. &nbsp;Whistleblowers are vital to maintaining organizational trust, but they too often pay a high price for their courage.&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Organisational trust is essential for businesses to thrive in today&apos;s environment. By creating an open and trusting culture, businesses encourage whistleblowers to come forward with important information at the earliest possible opportunity helping businesses avoid potentially damaging scandals and build a positive reputation with the public.&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Whistleblowers need to be able to trust the organisation and have confidence in the reporting policy, procedures and platforms. Without trust, employees may be reluctant to come forward with information about misconduct for fear of reprisal. This can create an environment where wrongdoing goes unchecked and employee morale suffers. &nbsp;</p>
