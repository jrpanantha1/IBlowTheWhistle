<p><strong>Improving whistleblower protection is very straightforward&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Whistleblower Protection is absolutely fundamental to every successful whistleblowing program, but one incident, or <u>perceived incident</u> of retaliation against a whistleblower or the suppression of concerns can have a catastrophic impact on the organization.</p>
<p><br>Whistleblowers are the single most important and effective means of uncovering wrongdoing but too often they face retaliation, discrimination and the suppression of their concerns. This &nbsp;puts the organisation at risk of fines, penalties and the loss of their most valuable asset - the staff.&nbsp;<br><br>In a world where the ethical conduct of the organisation is now an increasingly important consideration when deciding who to invest in, the protection of whistleblowers by the implementation of a trustworthy and comprehensive whistleblowing framework is essential. &nbsp;<br><br>It is unusual for only one person to be aware of a concern about actual or perceived wrongdoing. In most cases the whistleblower will have shared their concern with colleagues before making a formal report.&nbsp;<br><br>Implementing&nbsp;and communicating clear anti retaliation programmes, recognising the value of whistleblowers and celebrating whistleblowing can be daunting but with help it is easy and a cost effective way of protecting your organisation. Protection and anti-retaliation policy demonstrates commitment to providing a safe and welcoming environment for whistleblowers to speak up safely, reducing risks associated with whistleblowers reporting outside of the organisation.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Providing reassurance about how whistleblowers will be treated helps to support an open and transparent culture encouraging whistleblowers to come forward at the earliest possible opportunity.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>WhistleblowersUK have designed a simple 5 step programme to help organizations develop, demonstrate and promote positive whistleblower culture by setting and communicating clear standards for how whistleblowers and their concerns will be treated.</p>
