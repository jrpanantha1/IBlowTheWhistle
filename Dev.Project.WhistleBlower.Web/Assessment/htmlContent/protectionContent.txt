Protection</b></td></tr><tr><td colspan='3' style='text-align: justify;padding-top:10px'><p>Whistleblowing is the act of disclosing information about concerns or wrongdoing within an organization to the people who have the responsibility and authority to take action to rectify the problem.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>Speaking up about concerns is rarely optional, it is usually a professional obligation or responsibility which can become a source of conflict when people within the organisation attempt to silence the concerns. </p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>It is these negative experiences that undermine the trust and confidence of employees giving rise to the importance of embedding proper protection into the whistleblower framework. </p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>In recent years, many high-profile stories about the persecution of whistleblowers have emblazoned the front pages of the press driving down share prices and public confidence. These stories expose only some of the consequences of retaliation. </p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>For every whistleblower who experiences retaliation there are many good employees who say nothing and move on and make it more difficult to recruit and retain high calibre staff and increasingly to meet the stringent new global regulations that allow trade to continue. </p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>It is essential that organizations ensure that there are no personal and professional consequences for speaking out and that concerns and wrongdoing can be reported to those with the responsibility to take action.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>By the time that retaliation receives the attention of the board it is too often after things have gone badly wrong and the organization is confronted with public scrutiny following the termination, demotion, and harassment of the whistleblower.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>Around the world new laws are being rapidly introduced to protect people from retaliation and organisations who fail to adopt proactive whistleblowing practices are at increased risk of fines and penalties. </p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>We can help you find the right solution for your organisation helping you protect everyone. </p><p>&nbsp;</p></td></tr><tr><td colspan='3' style='padding-top: 15px;font-size: larger;'> <b>Your Score - ##score## out of a possible 10.</b></td></tr><tr><td colspan='3' style='padding-top: 15px;'>##scoreCalculation##</td></tr></table>
